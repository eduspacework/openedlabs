Our QA Process

A structured approach to software quality

We establish a clear testing process that aligns testing activities with your application, requirements, risks, and release objectives.

1

Understand

We review requirements, workflows, application architecture, user expectations, and quality risks.

2

Plan

We define test scenarios, coverage, environments, priorities, test data, and execution strategy.

3

Test

We execute planned tests, automate suitable scenarios, identify defects, and document clear evidence.

4

Validate

We verify fixes, perform regression testing, assess remaining risks, and provide clear quality reporting.
