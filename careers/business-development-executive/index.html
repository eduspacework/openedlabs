The Opportunity

Business development with a purpose.

openEd Labs works across Programming, Computer Science, Generative AI, emerging technologies and professional technical education.

We are looking for an independent business development professional who can help connect our offerings with schools, colleges, organisations and other potential clients.

This is a client-facing growth role focused on identifying opportunities, starting conversations, developing relationships and supporting conversions.

Your mission

Help openEd Labs reach more institutions, organisations and learners.

  • Generate qualified leads
  • Build new professional relationships
  • Promote relevant openEd Labs offerings
  • Develop institutional opportunities
  • Support client conversion and growth
What You Will Do

Your role in our growth.

You will work independently while collaborating with the openEd Labs team to identify, develop and convert new business opportunities.

01

Lead Generation

Research and identify potential schools, colleges, training institutes, organisations and other relevant prospects.

02

Outreach

Initiate professional conversations through email, phone, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and other appropriate communication channels.

03

Program Promotion

Introduce prospects to relevant openEd Labs training programs, technical education and professional learning opportunities.

04

Institutional Outreach

Connect with academic decision-makers, training coordinators, HR teams and organisational contacts.

05

Relationship Building

Develop professional relationships with prospects and maintain communication throughout the business development process.

06

Conversion & Follow-up

Follow up with qualified prospects and help convert opportunities into training engagements, collaborations or service requirements.
